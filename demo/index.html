What this is. A hobby experiment. The connections come from a real brain wiring map (FlyWire FAFB v783): the central-complex neurons flies use to steer toward a goal direction. The weights are fixed; only 141 numbers were trained with an evolution strategy (about a minute on a laptop). They map six game readings (ball position and velocity, paddle height) onto the input neurons, and read the paddle speed from the output neurons. Every signal has to pass through the hΔ intermediate neurons: silence them and the paddle stops. It is not a fly's mind. In our tests 4 of 5 trained networks won all 100 test matches against this opponent; the fifth lost most of them.
